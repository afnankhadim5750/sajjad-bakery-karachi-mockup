How a day runs here

  1. Morning

    The ovens are on

    The first bread of the day comes out of the oven, and the counter starts to fill.

  2. Midday

    The counter fills

    Breads, biscuits and the cake fridge — the full range, ready for the lunch-hour rush.

  3. Evening

    Hot counter, cake collections

    Samosas and pakoras come off the hot counter, and the day’s ordered cakes are collected for the night’s gatherings.

  4. Late night

    Still open

    The bakery stays open late, so the last customer of the night can still take bread home for tomorrow’s breakfast.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you deliver?

Yes — delivery is available. Ring 021 34970298 and ask about delivery to your block.

Can I order a cake by phone?

Yes. Ring the counter, tell us the date and how many people the cake should serve, and collect it from Javed Square — or ask for delivery.

What time do you close?

The bakery opens every day and stays open late. Closing times are not published, so ring ahead on the day to confirm.

Do you sell samosas and pakoras?

Yes — samosas and pakoras are on the hot counter, along with nimko for the chai table.

How much does a cake cost?

Prices vary by size and design. Ring the counter, describe the cake, and the bakery will quote you for it.

Where exactly are you?

Javed Square, Block 13-B, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi. Use the map above or the “Open in Google Maps” button for directions.
